M01 | Headcount
Definition: Active employees on the last day of the month (permanent and contract).
Formula: COUNT(DISTINCT employee_id) with hire_date <= month_end AND (exit_date IS NULL OR exit_date > month_end)
Source: fact_headcount_monthly / dim_employee
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month end
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: count
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Excludes CEO if you compare departments only

M02 | Hires
Definition: Employees whose hire_date falls in the month.
Formula: COUNT(employee_id) where hire_date in month
Source: fact_headcount_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: count
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Transfers are not hires

M03 | Exits
Definition: Employees whose exit_date falls in the month (resigned, terminated or contract ended).
Formula: COUNT(employee_id) where exit_date in month
Source: fact_headcount_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: count
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: 

M04 | Attrition rate (monthly)
Definition: Share of people who left in the month.
Formula: Exits / headcount_start x 100
Source: fact_headcount_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 10
Caveat: Very noisy for small departments

M05 | Attrition rate (rolling 12 months)
Definition: Leavers in the last 12 months divided by average headcount over the same months.
Formula: SUM(exits, 12m) / AVG(headcount_end, 12m) x 100
Source: fact_headcount_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: 12 months
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 10
Caveat: Use for headline reporting; monthly is too noisy

M06 | Voluntary attrition rate
Definition: Same as M05 but only resignations (voluntary_exits).
Formula: SUM(voluntary_exits, 12m) / AVG(headcount_end, 12m) x 100
Source: fact_headcount_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: 12 months
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 10
Caveat: 

M07 | Gross pay
Definition: Total earnings before deductions for the month.
Formula: SUM(gross_pay)
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Employee x month | Period: Month
Owner: Payroll | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Includes overtime and variable pay

M08 | Net pay
Definition: Take-home pay after employee deductions.
Formula: SUM(net_pay)
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Employee x month | Period: Month
Owner: Payroll | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: 

M09 | Payroll cost (employer cost)
Definition: Full cost of employees to the company: gross pay plus employer PF and ESI.
Formula: SUM(employer_cost)
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Employee x month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Must reconcile with ledger accounts 5001-5005

M10 | Cost per head
Definition: Average employer cost per paid employee.
Formula: SUM(employer_cost) / COUNT(DISTINCT employee_id)
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Part-month joiners lower the average

M11 | Overtime share
Definition: How much of payroll cost is overtime pay.
Formula: SUM(overtime_pay) / SUM(employer_cost) x 100
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: Payroll | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Only overtime-eligible roles earn overtime

M12 | Variable pay share
Definition: Bonus, incentive and award pay as a share of gross pay.
Formula: SUM(variable_pay) / SUM(gross_pay) x 100
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: Payroll | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: April bonus and quarter-end incentives create spikes

M13 | Attendance rate
Definition: Days present (half day counts 0.5) divided by scheduled working days.
Formula: (SUM(present_days) + 0.5 x SUM(half_days_unpaid)) / SUM(scheduled_days)
Source: fact_attendance_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: ratio
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Paid leave counts as not present

M14 | Late arrival rate
Definition: Late check-ins per present day.
Formula: SUM(late_arrivals) / SUM(present_days)
Source: fact_attendance_monthly
Grain: Department x month | Period: Month
Owner: HR Analytics | Unit: ratio
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Work-from-home days use web check-in time

M15 | Leave without pay days
Definition: Unpaid absence and unpaid leave days.
Formula: SUM(lop_days) in fact_payroll
Source: fact_payroll
Grain: Employee x month | Period: Month
Owner: Payroll | Unit: days
Minimum group size: 5
Caveat: Half-day unpaid counts 0.5

M16 | Revenue
Definition: Income booked in the ledger (accounts 4001-4004).
Formula: -SUM(signed_amount) for accounts 4001-4004
Source: fact_ledger
Grain: Account x month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Excludes GST (booked to 2200)

M17 | Net profit
Definition: Revenue minus all expenses (4xxx less 5xxx).
Formula: -SUM(signed_amount) for accounts 4xxx and 5xxx
Source: fact_ledger
Grain: Month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Interest income (4900) is included

M18 | Operating margin
Definition: Net profit as a share of revenue.
Formula: Net profit / Revenue x 100
Source: fact_ledger
Grain: Month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: 

M19 | Payroll as % of revenue
Definition: Share of revenue consumed by payroll cost.
Formula: Payroll cost / Revenue x 100
Source: fact_payroll + fact_ledger
Grain: Month | Period: Month
Owner: Finance | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Use ledger payroll accounts to avoid mismatch

M20 | Days sales outstanding (DSO)
Definition: Average days to collect: receivables divided by credit sales x days.
Formula: Closing receivables / revenue incl GST (last 90 days) x 90
Source: fact_invoice, fact_ledger
Grain: Month | Period: 90 days
Owner: Finance | Unit: days
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Carried-in invoices included

M21 | Overdue receivables
Definition: Unpaid invoice balance past due date at the report date.
Formula: SUM(balance_due) where due_date < report date and status not DRAFT/CANCELLED
Source: fact_invoice
Grain: Client x date | Period: Point in time
Owner: Finance | Unit: INR
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: 

M22 | Task completion rate
Definition: Tasks done divided by tasks planned in the sprint (cancelled tasks excluded).
Formula: COUNT(status = Done) / COUNT(status != Cancelled)
Source: fact_task
Grain: Team x sprint | Period: Sprint
Owner: Engineering Ops | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 5 tasks
Caveat: Sample feed until the live board is ready

M23 | On-time delivery rate
Definition: Done tasks completed on or before the sprint end date.
Formula: COUNT(completed_on_time = Y) / COUNT(status = Done)
Source: fact_task
Grain: Team x sprint | Period: Sprint
Owner: Engineering Ops | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 5 tasks
Caveat: 

M24 | Velocity
Definition: Story points completed in the sprint.
Formula: SUM(story_points) where status = Done
Source: fact_task
Grain: Team x sprint | Period: Sprint
Owner: Engineering Ops | Unit: points
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Do not compare across teams

M25 | Average cycle time
Definition: Days from start to completion for done tasks.
Formula: AVG(completed_date - started_date)
Source: fact_task
Grain: Team x sprint | Period: Sprint
Owner: Engineering Ops | Unit: days
Minimum group size: 5 tasks
Caveat: Negative values are data errors

M26 | Anomaly flag rate
Definition: Share of scored records flagged by the AI anomaly service.
Formula: COUNT(flagged = Y) / COUNT(*)
Source: fact_anomaly
Grain: Source x month | Period: Month
Owner: AI/ML | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Flags are leads, not proof

M27 | High-risk share
Definition: Share of employees in the High attrition-risk band.
Formula: COUNT(risk_band = High) / COUNT(*)
Source: fact_attrition_risk
Grain: Department | Period: Point in time
Owner: AI/ML | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: 10
Caveat: Show by group only; never rank named people

M28 | Forecast error (MAPE)
Definition: Average absolute percentage error of headcount forecasts versus actuals.
Formula: AVG(ABS(actual - forecast) / actual) x 100
Source: fact_forecast vs fact_headcount
Grain: Department x month | Period: Rolling
Owner: AI/ML | Unit: percent
Minimum group size: NOT SPECIFIED
Caveat: Needs actuals for the forecast months
